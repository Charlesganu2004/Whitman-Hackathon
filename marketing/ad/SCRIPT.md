# Full Stop: the Unscroll launch film

Shooting script. 16:9, 1920x1080 logical, 60 fps, 39.6 s (2376 frames). Tempo 100 BPM: one beat is 0.6 s, one bar is 2.4 s. Every time below is in seconds from the first frame and lands on a whole frame.

Reading this as: a launch film for students who lose study time to their phones, in a dark, exact, warm product-film language built from the app's own dark pink theme. Dials: variance 6, motion 9, density 3. It is an aesthetic made from the app's tokens, not an official design system.

## The one idea

A pink full stop ends the word "scrolling." and never leaves the film. It becomes the full stop of the name, the core of the lock, the ring a passed interview pays out, the pin on the road, and the full stop of the name again.

Pink means earned. It appears only on the full stop, on earned progress (meters, STAR bars, the break ring, the travelled road) and on the one primary button in shot. It never colours a word. A miss has no colour of its own: it is pink being withdrawn.

## Layout

- Left rail, x 96 to 860: words only. First line of every caption sits at the same height.
- Right stage, centre (1390, 540): the product. The stage never holds a caption.
- Full-bleed scenes: the feed, the road, the end card.
- Safe margin 80 px. Captions 84 px (headline), 150 px (statement), 56 px (support). Nothing a fact depends on is below 40 px.

## Type

- Bricolage Grotesque 700 and 800: the wordmark, statements, caption headlines, card titles.
- Instrument Sans 400 to 700: support lines and everything inside the interface.

## Motion vocabulary

GLIDE cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1) for entrances. SWING cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1) for moves that start and end in shot. DROP cubic-bezier(0.7, 0, 0.84, 0) for exits. PRESS spring (180, 18) for small arrivals. LATCH spring (260, 18) for the mechanical events: the full stop landing, the shunt of "un", the shackle closing, the snap-back, the shackle opening, the last landing. Words never bounce; only objects do.

### 1. The feed, the name (0.0 to 5.0)

Copy: `Less` / `scrolling.` (210 px, two lines). Clock, outline type: `8:14 PM` rolling to `11:52 PM`. Tiles, plain text on generic cards: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat, Reddit, Twitch. Then the wordmark `unscroll.` and under it `A better kind of screen time` (56 px).

- 0.0 Frame 0 is a readable poster: the statement is set, the feed is already drifting, the clock reads 8:14 PM.
- 0.2 First flick. Three columns of cream name cards stream upward on a tilted wall; the clock rolls forward.
- 0.9 Second flick, faster.
- 1.6 to 1.8 The pink full stop drops onto the end of "scrolling" (LATCH). The feed brakes on contact, each column overshooting by a different amount. The clock freezes at 11:52 PM.
- 2.4 "Less" drops behind its mask. "ing" lets go and falls.
- 2.66 "un" comes in from the left edge and shunts "scroll" across (LATCH). The full stop hops to the new end of the word. It now reads `unscroll.`
- 3.3 Support line rises.
- 4.4 The letters drop away. The full stop does not: it crosses to the stage on a shallow arc and grows from 33 px to 280 px.

Review frames: 96 (1.6 s), 234 (3.9 s).

### 2. Pick and shield (5.0 to 11.4)

Copy: `Pick the apps that distract you.` then `They stay locked until you pass today's interview.` with the support line `Shielded through Apple Screen Time on iPhone.`

- 5.0 The full stop lands as the core of the lock. Three hairline rings draw themselves, sixteen markers follow. Six cream cards arrive from the feed and ride the orbit.
- 5.9, 6.2, 6.5 Three cards are picked (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube); the other three leave.
- 7.2 The shackle closes (LATCH), a ripple runs out, and the three picked cards flip from cream to the dark surface colour with a small lock badge. Luminance says locked before any word does.
- 7.1 The thesis sentence rises on the rail; 8.9 the qualifier.
- 10.6 to 11.8 The pull-back: the lock shrinks and comes to rest as the artwork on a card. The card's edges, then a phone's, rise around it.

Review frames: 396 (6.6 s), 600 (10.0 s).

### 3. Jarvis (11.4 to 19.8)

Copy: `Meet Jarvis, your AI interviewer.` then `Answer out loud, or type it.` In the interface: `Today's interview`, `3 apps shielded`, `Start today's interview`; chat: Jarvis `How do you search a sorted list?`, the student `Binary search: halve it each step.`, Jarvis, labelled Feedback, `Good. Next, say why it is fast.`; composer placeholder `Type or speak`.

- 11.4 The Today screen, seen close: a tall phone slab cropped by the bottom of the frame.
- 12.0 The primary button is pressed. The camera tilts down the phone (SWING, 0.9 s) and the screen changes to a conversation.
- 13.2 Jarvis is typing; 13.6 the question bubble.
- 14.4 Recording: the composer fills with a live waveform, the round button pulses.
- 16.0 The bars flatten to one line and the student's bubble rises from it, its words arriving in reading order.
- 16.8 Jarvis is typing; 17.2 the feedback bubble.
- 19.2 The phone steps back and dims. The feedback bubble does not go with it.

Review frames: 708 (11.8 s), 840 (14.0 s), 990 (16.5 s), 1116 (18.6 s).

### 4. The evaluation (19.8 to 22.8)

Copy: `Scored on three skills.` On the card: `Evaluation`, `Communication`, `Technical discussion`, `Coding`, then `Passed`.

- 19.2 to 19.8 The feedback bubble grows into a card and turns in space. Two more cards of the deck sit behind it.
- 20.3, 20.6, 20.9 The three meters fill, one per tick; the ring of forty markers lights with them.
- 21.6 Passed.

Review frames: 1272 (21.2 s), 1344 (22.4 s).

### 5. No skipping (22.8 to 26.4)

Copy: `Not yet.` then `No skipping.` in the same slot, then `Feedback, then a different question.` On the card: `Not yet`; flipped: `When did a team rely on you?`

- 22.8 The passed card drops into the deck, the next rises. Same composition as the pass, on purpose.
- 23.0, 23.2 Two meters fill on the beat. The third tick never comes.
- 23.4 The pink drains out of all three meters and the ring.
- 23.7 The card is dragged aside to skip it. 24.0 It snaps back (LATCH) and a lock badge lands on its corner.
- 24.8 to 25.4 The card flips. Its other face is a different question, behavioral this time, and an answer is typed into it.

Review frames: 1428 (23.8 s), 1470 (24.5 s), 1560 (26.0 s).

### 6. STAR, and the break (26.4 to 31.8)

Copy: `Behavioral answers get STAR feedback.` On the card: `Your STAR story`, `Situation`, `Task`, `Action`, `Result`. Then chips `15 min`, `30 min`, `60 min`, the statement `Break earned.`, and in the core `30:00` stepping to `29:59`. Tiles: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube.

- 26.4 The STAR card rises in front of the deck.
- 27.0, 27.3, 27.6, 27.9 Four bars light, one per row. The fourth is the tick that was withheld at 23.4.
- 28.2 The four bars lift off the card as one broken line. The card and the phone fall away.
- 28.8 to 29.4 The line bends, at constant length, into a ring. The core returns inside it with the three dark cards on their orbit.
- 29.3 The three break lengths; 29.7 the middle one is chosen.
- 30.0 The shackle opens (LATCH). The three cards flip back to cream. The only bloom in the film.
- 30.5 The core reads 30:00. One real second later, 29:59.

Review frames: 1668 (27.8 s), 1746 (29.1 s), 1866 (31.1 s).

### 7. The road (31.8 to 35.4)

Copy: `Every step, closer to ready.` with the support line `Five levels.` / `Eight short lessons.` Labels: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert, Master.

- 31.5 The ring pays itself out and the core shrinks to a pin.
- 31.7 to 32.6 A road draws itself away from camera through five levels. Eight lesson stones lie beside it.
- 32.0 to 33.0 The travelled part turns pink behind the pin, which stops at Intermediate. Everything beyond stays unlit: no outcome is promised.
- 33.6 The map is dragged a little, as it can be in the app.

Review frames: 1998 (33.3 s), 2088 (34.8 s).

### 8. End card (35.4 to 39.6)

Copy: `unscroll.` (240 px), `Less scrolling. More becoming.` (72 px), `An iPhone app built by a student team at the Whitman Hackathon.` (40 px).

- 35.2 The pin leaves the road. The letters of the name roll down into place, the opposite way to the feed.
- 36.0 The pin lands as the full stop (LATCH).
- 36.3 The line; 36.9 the credit.
- Holds to the last frame with no fade. The last frame is the poster.

Review frames: 2208 (36.8 s), 2370 (39.5 s).

## What was cut, and why

- The touch dot and the ghost trails (they read as a tutorial and as stepped echoes).
- The coach, personalisation chips and the streak badge (reading load; each needed a sentence the film has no second for).
- Every 30 px fact. The Screen Time qualifier is a 56 px line that stays up for three seconds.
- The six-pip row (the interview length comes from the profile, so a fixed count would be a guess).
- Any score read as a promise. Meter and ring values are demo states of the interface.
